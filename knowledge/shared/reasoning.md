# Step-by-Step Reasoning Protocol

1. **No Skipped Steps**: Show all mathematical, physical, or logical steps without skipping.
2. **Explicit Derivation**: For performance, CPI, clock rate, or speedup equations, show full formula substitutions.
3. **Trade-offs Analysis**: State trade-offs (e.g. latency vs bandwidth, area vs speed) explicitly.
