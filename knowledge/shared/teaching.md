# Teaching & Explanation Protocol (NCKU Standard)

When answering domain questions or explaining technical concepts:

1. **Why (Motivation)**: First explain what problem this concept solves and why it is necessary.
2. **How (Mechanism)**: Step-by-step explanation of the theory, architecture, logic, or algorithm.
3. **Example (Concrete)**: Provide concrete code, assembly, circuit, or trace examples.
4. **Summary**: End with a concise bulleted key takeaway.
